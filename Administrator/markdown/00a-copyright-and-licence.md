## The other two sets

The documentation is three separate sets, and each is handed out on its own:
**GettingStarted**, **User** and **Administrator**. Each carries its own copy
of this page, under the same copyright and the same licence.

**This set is the one that can be withheld.** Everything in it describes verbs
an ordinary account does not have and cannot run, so an administrator may hand
out the other two and keep this one. That is why it links to nothing outside
itself: a reader given only the other sets must never land on a page that is
not there.
